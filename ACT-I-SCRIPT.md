# ACT I — First Act Screenplay Sample

INT. BLACKSITE LABORATORY - NIGHT

Dark red emergency lights pulse through a sealed research facility.

A metal door tears open. Armed soldiers storm the room.

Inside are large glass tanks. Each contains a black mass of living matter, shimmering like liquid shadow.

DR. MIRA SOL, 34, steps forward, her hands trembling.

MIRA
Don't open it. The signal is changing again.

GENERAL ROOK, 52, stands at the head of the chamber, expression cold and resolute.

ROOK
We're past the point of caution.

A mechanical cutter pierces the tank.

The black matter rises with impossible speed.

The creature inside is not humanoid. It is a blend of muscle, bone, darkness, and liquid material.

It sees them.

The form stretches and multiplies. A monstrous body blooms into several versions at once.

SOLDIER 1
Fire! Fire!

The soldiers open fire. Bullets hit the mass and are absorbed. The entity reforms instantly.

The creature lunges.

A soldier is swallowed by a dark tendril. Others scream as the thing tears through the chamber.

The glass bursts. The room convulses around them.

MIRA
(terrified)
It's not just growing. It's multiplying.

ROOK
Then we weaponize it.

The creature turns toward him.

A voice, not spoken but heard in every mind, rattles through the walls.

PRIME INFINITYSOMBIOTE
THE HOST HAS ARRIVED.

The darkness surges forward.

CUT TO BLACK.

CUT TO:

EXT. CITY ROOFTOP - DAWN

Lucas stands alone on a rooftop. The city below is alive, but he is not.

He stares at the horizon.

A dark speck appears in the distance, spreading impossibly over the skyline.

Lucas looks up and mutters in disbelief.

LUCAS
No. No, not again.

A title card appears.

INFINITYSOMBIOTES

CUT TO:

INT. SECRET MILITARY FACILITY - LATER

Lucas stands in a briefing room among armed officials and scientists. Video footage of the creature plays on a wall.

ROOK
The Infinitysombiotes were found beneath the Arctic ice. We are not dealing with a weapon. We are looking at a species-level evolutionary design.

MIRA
It amplifies more than physicality. It amplifies the host's core traits. Ego. Instinct. Desire. Potential.

LUCAS
So what you're saying is this thing turns people into gods.

MIRA
Worse.

ROOK
We don't need to be afraid. We need to be ready.

LUCAS
Ready for what? A catastrophe?

ROOK
For supremacy.

The room grows still.

Lucas stares at Rook.

LUCAS
That's not peace. That's domination.

Rook turns away, already looking toward the next step.

ROOK
This is bigger than any of us.

A montage begins: black site chambers, test pods, subjects, transformation, shockwaves, emergency lights, all moving rapidly.

MIRA (V.O.)
The more symbiotes a host bonds with, the more powerful they become. The threshold is unstable. No one understands the final stage yet.

Lucas especially sees a test subject in a chamber, screaming as a black organic mass floods into him.

The body transforms. Steel walls tear apart. The host becomes a swirling embodiment of living darkness.

LUCAS
(quietly)
What happens when it stops being a host and starts being a god?

Mira looks at him.

MIRA
Then it becomes the beginning of something new.

The act ends on Lucas, now realizing the true danger is not the creature alone — it's the people trying to turn it into a weapon.
