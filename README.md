## Core Concept
The Infinitysombiotes are a species of alien symbiote organisms that multiply the intensity of a host's power to absurd, godlike levels. Their effects are not just physical. They amplify the host's core traits, instincts, ego, and consciousness to an almost incomprehensible degree. A human host can become a titan-like force, but the more power they absorb, the more dangerous and unstable the transformation becomes.

This is a dark and epic story about corruption, power, grief, and the question of whether humanity can evolve without losing itself.

## Logline
When a buried alien organism known as the Infinitysombiotes is awakened beneath the Earth, a rogue military program weaponizes it into a terrifying army of godlike hosts. Each symbiote multiplies the host's power beyond human limits, turning ordinary soldiers into titan-level monsters. A haunted former operative and a brilliant scientist must stop the final fusion before the original symbiote intelligence completes its transformation of humanity into a new race of cosmic conquerors.
