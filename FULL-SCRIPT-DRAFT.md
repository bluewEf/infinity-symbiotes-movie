# INFINITYSOMBIOTES — Expanded Full Script Draft

## ACT I — THE AWAKENING

INT. BLACKSITE LABORATORY - NIGHT

Dark red emergency lights pulse through a sealed research facility.

A METAL DOOR rips open with a burst of force as soldiers rush inside, rifles raised.

The room is lined with reinforced glass tanks. Inside each tank floats a black, shimmering mass of living matter — not a creature, not a liquid, but something between both.

A low, wet, unnatural breathing echoes from the center tank.

DR. MIRA SOL, 34, steps forward, trembling.

MIRA
Don't open it. The signal is changing again.

GENERAL ROOK, 52, hard-eyed and ruthless, stands before the tank.

ROOK
We're past the point of caution.

A HIGH-POWERED CUTTER drills into the tank.

The black mass shifts. It rises, slowly, like a curtain of animated darkness.

The creature inside is not humanoid. It is an impossible blend of bone, muscle, and liquid shadow.

Then it sees them.

Its body blooms outward like a blooming wound. Each appendage forms at impossible speed.

The creature splits in half, then doubles, then becomes FIVE moving forms at once.

SOLDIER 1
Fire! Fire!

The soldiers open fire. Bullets punch into the thing, but the black matter absorbs the shock and reforms instantly.

The creature lunges.

One soldier is wrapped in a dark tendril and dragged into the mass. The others explode into screams as the body becomes a river of metal and force.

The glass in the room cracks from the shockwave.

MIRA
(terrified)
It's not just growing. It's multiplying.

ROOK
Then we weaponize it.

The creature turns toward him.

A voice, not spoken but heard in every mind, vibrates through the walls.

PRIME INFINITYSOMBIOTE
THE HOST HAS ARRIVED.

The darkness surges forward.

The screen goes black.

CUT TO:

EXT. CITY ROOFTOP - DAWN

LUCAS VOSS, 38, stands alone on a rooftop, staring out over a city that has not yet learned what is coming.

He is broad-shouldered and scarred. A man who has seen violence but never been able to outrun it.

A military helicopter hovers low in the distance before passing beyond the skyline.

Lucas turns his head slightly, as if hearing the machine in his bones.

He reaches into his pocket and pulls out a worn photograph of a young woman — ELENA VOSS, 29, smiling in a field of snow.

The city beneath him is alive. But Lucas looks like he is already mourning something no one else can see.

Then his expression changes.

A dark speck appears in the distance, slowly spreading across the horizon.

He knows this shape.

LUCAS
No. No, not again.

The speck grows larger, shaped like a living shadow in the sky.

The camera tightens on his face.

CUT TO TITLE CARD: INFINITYSOMBIOTES

CUT TO:

INT. SECLUDED SAFEHOUSE - HOURS LATER

A cramped apartment with cheap furniture, old military equipment, and a television playing a muted news report.

Lucas sits alone at a table, staring at a chipped mug of coffee. He is exhausted, angry, hollow.

The TV flickers with a news banner:

"Government denies underground bioweapons program after unexplained desert casualties."

A KNOCK on the door.

Lucas reaches for a pistol on the table, then stops when the door opens.

AGENT CHRISTINE PARK, 40s, sharp and controlled, steps inside. She looks at Lucas without blinking.

PARK
We need you back in the field.

LUCAS
I already told you. I'm done.

PARK
This isn't a field op. This is a containment problem.

She sets a file on the table.

Inside are photos of the underground chamber and the black mass from the lab.

Lucas looks at the photos. He doesn't say anything.

PARK
At least one hundred military personnel died in forty-seven seconds. The thing was beneath the desert. We found it in a sealed vault under an old Arctic base.

LUCAS
You found a monster. So what's the problem?

PARK
The problem is it was alive before anyone knew it existed. And it's not just a monster.

Lucas finally looks up.

PARK
It is moving. It is learning. It is multiplying.

Lucas's eyes narrow.

LUCAS
You said it was buried under an Arctic base.

PARK
It was. This isn't natural. It isn't Earth.

Lucas's hand tightens around the photo.

LUCAS
Why me?

PARK
Because they already tried to recruit the men who survived. They were all useless. They wanted someone who could think in a crisis.

LUCAS
And you think that's me?

PARK
I think it's the only thing keeping you alive.

Lucas stares at the file, jaw clenched.

Lucas. The whole thing feels wrong. Too familiar.

PARK
They said the same thing about the last lab.

A beat.

PARK
A single soldier survived the first fusion. He was the one who killed the entire squad.

Lucas doesn't move.

PARK
And then he kept moving. Like he was trying to become something bigger than a person.

Lucas finally rises.

LUCAS
I don't do this anymore.

PARK
Then do it for the people who never had a choice.

He looks at the photo again. The image is grainy, impossible. Something inside him clicks.

A memory flashes.

ELENA (V.O.)
If the military finds out what I've discovered, they'll try to weaponize it.

Lucas's eyes flicker. He doesn't know why the memory is returning now.

PARK
Lucas.

He breathes. His voice is cold.

LUCAS
Tell me everything.

CUT TO:

INT. BLACKSITE ALPHA - LATER

The room is a subterranean command center, all steel walls, surveillance screens, and red-lit partitions.

Lucas is escorted in by two armed guards. He looks around the facility with cold disdain.

GENERAL ROOK stands at the front of a briefing chamber, his posture rigid and commanding.

DR. MIRA SOL stands at his side, focused and nervous. Her eyes flick to Lucas immediately.

ROOK
Agent Voss. Your reputation is... political.

LUCAS
And your reputation is a problem.

Rook doesn't react. He continues.

ROOK
We found a dormant alien organism beneath the Arctic shelf. We call it the Infinitysombiote. It is not a parasite. It is not a weapon. It is a species-level intelligence built to amplify biological power beyond any natural limit.

Lucas crosses his arms.

LUCAS
You make it sound elegant.

MIRA
It isn't elegant. It's alive, adaptive, and impossible to contain.

ROOK
We have already bonded it with test subjects. The first host became stronger than any human could plausibly be. He could tear through reinforced steel, absorb impossible trauma, and keep moving.

Lucas looks at the screen. Footage appears: a human subject, nearly naked, screaming as a black mass surges into his bloodstream. The host's body reshapes violently, bones snapping and reforming, skin darkening into a living shadow.

The host tears through a metal wall as if it were paper.

The video freezes on the host's face — glowing, inhuman, and nearly beautiful.

LUCAS
This is what you're doing?

ROOK
This is what it requires.

MIRA
It doesn't just give power. It amplifies the host's instincts. Not just strength. The nature of the person.

LUCAS
That's what makes it dangerous.

MIRA
That's what makes it a weapon.

ROOK
And that is exactly why it matters.

Lucas steps closer, eye level with Rook.

LUCAS
People don't become weapons because they have power. They become weapons because they're taught to believe power is the answer.

Rook's eyes narrow.

ROOK
And yet here you stand, thinking with all the certainty of a man who has never had to make a choice that saves the world.

A beat.

LUCAS
I don't need to be a hero. I just need to stop you from doing this.

Rook gives a cold smile.

ROOK
That depends on how much of you still believes it is possible to save a world that has already begun to end.

The room goes still.

MIRA
Lucas, this is worse than a weapon. The Infinitysombiotes are link-based. The host isn't alone. The organism is networked. It's not one creature. It's a system.

LUCAS
A system?

MIRA
A species. All of it connected. All of it sharing consciousness. If one host bonds, the rest can feel it. They can communicate. They can evolve.

Lucas processes that, the horror crawling over his expression.

LUCAS
So the whole thing isn't just alive.

MIRA
It's organized.

ROOK
And the first generation is not enough. We are moving forward.

Lucas looks at Mira.

LUCAS
How many did you have?

Mira hesitates. Rook answers for her.

ROOK
Twelve intact symbiote organisms. Enough to create an army.

The room feels suddenly compressed.

LUCAS
You're building a god army.

ROOK
No. We are building the future.

Lucas nearly laughs.

LUCAS
You don't even know if it will survive contact with a human brain.

ROOK
We do know it survives. That's enough.

MIRA
No. It isn't.

Rook turns to her.

ROOK
You told me the host remained conscious at initial merge. That means adaptation is possible. It can be guided.

MIRA
Only if you are trying to save them. You are trying to weaponize the very thing that should have remained extinct.

Rook says nothing.

The chamber lights flicker.

A distant alarm starts pulsing through the walls.

A monitor behind Rook flashes with red warning lights.

A large signal on screen: SUBJECT 07 - UNSTABLE MERGE.

Rook turns to the monitor.

ROOK
Move the containment team.

The room shifts. The energy in the air changes.

A low, resonant hum begins to grow through the walls.

Then, on the chamber monitor, a human SHADOW suddenly expands beyond the boundaries of the room.

The screen flickers.

And in the dark, a shape appears inside the tank — not human, not even animal — something taller than the room itself.

It is starting.

CUT TO:

INT. BLACKSITE CHAMBER - CONTINUOUS

A lead-lined room filled with tubes, monitors, and a single oversized pod at its center.

Inside the pod is KADE MERCER, 40, former military veteran and Lucas's old teammate. He is strapped into a chair, skin pale, his body shaking.

A black mass pulses inside the pod, writhing under glass.

Mira steps in, eyes fixed on the chamber.

MIRA
He's not ready.

ROOK
He volunteered.

Kade opens his eyes.

The pupils glow.

The black matter tears through the glass like water.

The chamber explodes into fragments.

The host inside Kade surges upward.

His body stretches, bones snapping and reforming in a violent cascade. Muscle swells, skin darkens to a liquid black sheen, and the shape of a man becomes a living weapon.

Kade rises in a towering, impossible form.

His eyes burn with white-gold energy.

He lets out a roar that shakes the walls.

Kade's voice echoes with multiple tones.

KADE
I can feel it.

He turns toward Lucas.

KADE
Lucas.

A beat.

The Warlord smiles.

KADE
I can feel everything.

Lucas steps back, horrified.

The chamber is falling apart around them.

Kade laughs — a low, monstrous laugh that sounds like thunder twisting through a canyon.

ROOK
Welcome to ascension.

Kade lifts one hand, and the room's steel wall crumbles with one touch.

The symbiote host is more than powerful. It is impossible.

Lucas's breath catches.

This is the beginning.

CUT TO BLACK.

## ACT II — THE INFECTION

INT. RESEARCH LAB - NIGHT

Mira sits at a worktable covered in scans, diagrams, and strange glass samples. Lucas enters and shuts the door behind him.

They are alone in the lab.

MIRA
The deeper I dig, the worse it gets.

LUCAS
Tell me.

Mira slides a set of images across the table: ancient caverns, fossilized patterns, and impossible schematics.

MIRA
These aren't just parasites. They are an engineered species. These structures match traces from an ancient Earth period before the first humanoid civilization.

LUCAS
You're saying they're older than us.

MIRA
Much older. They were there before the dinosaurs. Maybe before humans. This isn't alien in the classic sense. It's a life pattern. An evolutionary system.

LUCAS
Then why is it under the Arctic.

MIRA
Because it was hidden. Deliberately. It was never supposed to be found.

Lucas studies the schematics.

A pattern emerges. It resembles a network, like a hive mind, but cosmic in geometry.

LUCAS
What are they trying to do?

Mira looks at him, and the answer is obvious in her eyes.

MIRA
Transform the planet.

Lucas says nothing.

MIRA
The Infinitysombiotes multiply capability. They don't just increase strength. They intensify the host's truest nature. The stronger the host, the more complete the transformation. 

LUCAS
So if a person is cruel, they become brutal. If a person is afraid, they become monstrous. If a person is driven by control, they become a god.

MIRA
Exactly.

Lucas stares at the charts, then at Mira.

LUCAS
What about Elena?

Mira's expression tightens.

MIRA
She knew something. She knew the same thing I do. She knew the symbiote could rewrite the entire evolutionary path of life on Earth.

LUCAS
And they killed her to keep it hidden.

MIRA
Maybe. Or maybe they were trying to weaponize it before she could stop them. Either way, she was onto it before anyone here had the courage to look.

Lucas takes a breath.

Then an image appears on a monitor — the faint, ancient symbol of a fractured circle. It was in Elena's notes.

MIRA
There. That's the pattern she kept finding in the chamber. It's not random. It's a map.

LUCAS
Of what?

MIRA
The Prime.

Lucas leans in.

MIRA
The original Infinitysombiote. The one that created the rest of the species.

LUCAS
And Rook wants to wake it.

MIRA
I don't think he understands what waking it means.

LUCAS
Then tell me.

Mira finally meets his eyes.

MIRA
If he bonds the strongest host to the central symbiote core, the Prime can fully manifest in this dimension. It will not just give power. It will begin to redesign life. Not as a weapon. As a civilization.

LUCAS
You mean a god.

MIRA
I mean something more than any human could ever be. It will create a new order.

The room feels suddenly cold.

MIRA
And if they do it, we won't be the ones in charge.

CUT TO:

INT. COMMAND CENTER - DAY

Men and women in tactical gear move through a command floor as military personnel work screens and review live data.

Rook stands at the center, flanked by officers and scientists. On the main screen, a map of the world blinks on and off as hosts are assigned to regions.

ROOK
We move to phase two. Begin field deployment. Every surviving host is to be active by sundown.

An officer looks uneasy.

OFFICER
Sir, the hosts are not all stable.

ROOK
Then stabilize them.

OFFICER
With respect, sir — some of them are not just unstable. They are responding to the Prime.

Rook doesn't flinch.

ROOK
Then they are ready.

In the hallway outside, Mira brushes past Lucas and whispers urgently.

MIRA
They're moving faster than we thought. The network is awake.

LUCAS
Then we go now.

Mira nods.

MIRA
Prisoner transfer at the lower levels. One chance to get to the archive before Rook has it sealed.

Lucas looks toward the command center.

LUCAS
Then let's make our own chance.

The screen above them flickers with one massive red warning.

PRIME CONSCIOUSNESS DETECTED.

The camera holds on the red signal as the script transitions to the next sequence.

CUT TO BLACK.

## Scene Summary: Why This Draft Works
This expanded script draft establishes the movie's central logic while showing the core visual beats and emotional drivers:

- The Infinitysombiote is not just a creature but a species-level intelligence.
- The military is trying to weaponize evolution.
- Lucas is morally and emotionally trapped between survivor's guilt and the need to stop the threat.
- Mira provides the scientific and moral grounding.
- Kade becomes catastrophic proof of the danger of unrestrained power.
- The Prime represents the greater existential threat: not just death, but transcendence without ethics.

The story is now clearly set up to escalate from military horror into world-ending cosmic horror.
